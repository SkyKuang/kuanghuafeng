01 / INTRODUCTION

About me

I am an AI Researcher at ByteDance, working on multimodal generative models for image and video synthesis. I received my Ph.D. in Artificial Intelligence from Xiamen University in 2024, where I was advised by Prof. Rongrong Ji.

My current work explores diffusion and autoregressive generative modeling, as well as low-bit quantization, sparsification and caching for efficient deployment. Earlier, I investigated adversarial robustness, explainable and trustworthy machine learning, and multimodal representation learning.

I enjoy connecting theoretical insights with real-world large-scale systems — turning research ideas into practical AI capabilities.
